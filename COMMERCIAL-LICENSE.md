# AIFP-4 Commercial License Framework

This file describes the commercial licensing structure. It is not an offer and does not replace a signed agreement.

A written commercial license from AiFinPay is required for any of the following:

- production commercial use of the reference implementation;
- hosted or managed AIFP-4 services;
- use by a payment provider, bank, fintech, marketplace, enterprise treasury platform, or virtual-asset provider in commercial operations;
- resale, sublicensing, white-labeling, or embedding in a third-party commercial product;
- operation of a competing treasury, routing, settlement, or partner network using substantial AIFP-4 reference code;
- use of official trademarks, logos, certification marks, or partner badges;
- access to proprietary enterprise modules, partner adapters, jurisdiction packs, compliance integrations, managed routing, support, SLAs, or the AIFP Global Settlement Network.

## Expected agreement sections

A production agreement should cover:

- licensed legal entity and affiliates;
- territories and jurisdictions;
- permitted products and deployment model;
- transaction and customer scope;
- software, patent, trademark, and certification rights;
- fees, minimum commitments, volume tiers, caps, and partner revenue share;
- information-security requirements;
- audit and conformance testing;
- financial-regulatory responsibility matrix;
- data protection and subprocessors;
- support, service levels, incident response, and business continuity;
- indemnities, liability limitations, insurance, termination, and migration;
- restrictions on reverse engineering of proprietary modules, credential sharing, circumvention, and unauthorized network access.

## Open specification boundary

A company may independently implement the public AIFP-4 specification under the specification license. It may not use protected marks, claim certification, access private partner infrastructure, or copy source-available reference code outside its license.

## Contact

Commercial rights should be requested through AiFinPay's official corporate contact channel. Only a written agreement signed by an authorized AiFinPay representative grants commercial, network, trademark, certification, or enterprise rights.
